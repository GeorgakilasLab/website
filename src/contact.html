Athena Research Center

Information Management Systems Institute (IMSI)

About: ATHENA Research Center is a leading Greek research institute specializing in Information and Communication Technologies (ICT), artificial intelligence, data science, and computational sciences. Established in 2003, ATHENA conducts fundamental and applied research, develops advanced digital technologies and research infrastructures, and promotes knowledge transfer and innovation. Through its research institutes and interdisciplinary units, the Center addresses scientific and societal challenges across diverse domains, including healthcare, life sciences, industry, culture, and sustainable development, fostering collaboration between academia, industry, and the broader research community.
