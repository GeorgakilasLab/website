Academic Year 2026-2027

About: Georgakilas Lab is pleased to announce a new collection of MSc thesis projects for the academic year 20266-2027. Interested students are invited to explore the available thesis topics and contact us to discuss potential research directions, project requirements, and supervision opportunities.

DAMOCLES Project Launched

Dynamic Modelling of Biological Systems for Enhanced Insights in Cancer Research and Therapy

About: e are thrilled to announce the launch of our latest initiative under Archimedes Unit of Athena Research Center, DAMOCLES! This groundbreaking project marks a significant milestone in our journey at GeorgakilasLab, pushing the boundaries of scRNA-seq processing and interpretation. DAMOCLES is a research project dedicated to advancing the computational understanding of complex biological systems through mathematical modeling, artificial intelligence, and single-cell omics analysis. Focusing on Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia (CLL), the project investigates the dynamic evolution of the tumor microenvironment and the interactions between heterogeneous cellular populations. By integrating high-dimensional single-cell transcriptomic data with computational modeling approaches, DAMOCLES aims to establish a framework for constructing and analyzing dynamic models of cellular systems. Particular emphasis is placed on investigating system stability, bifurcation behavior, and cellular trajectories, supporting a deeper understanding of cancer progression and potentially informing future therapeutic research.
